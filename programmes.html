Entry & Admissions

What you'll need to apply.

Our admissions process is designed to be clear and straightforward. Here's how it works — from enquiry to enrolment.

01

Submit your application

Complete the online application form with your personal details, academic history, and programme preference.

02

Provide supporting documents

Upload your academic transcripts, English proficiency evidence, and identification. International applicants may also need a visa eligibility check.

03

Application review

Our admissions team reviews your application and may invite you to an interview or request further information. You'll hear from us within five working days.

04

Offer and enrolment

Successful applicants receive an offer of place. Confirm your place, arrange tuition payment, and complete enrolment before your programme begins.

General requirements

A recognised secondary school qualification for undergraduate study (NCEA Level 3 or international equivalent)
A relevant bachelor's degree for postgraduate programmes
Evidence of English proficiency (IELTS 6.0+ or equivalent) for applicants whose first language is not English
Basic literacy and numeracy for most practical and short courses
