Welcome from Our President

A warm welcome to DameCooper Institute.

Dear students, faculty, staff, and visitors,

Welcome to DameCooper Institute of Higher Learning — a community where curiosity is celebrated, where rigorous scholarship meets practical experience, and where every student is supported to reach their full potential.

For over three decades, we have been committed to academic excellence, to fostering intellectual growth, and to preparing graduates who go on to shape their industries, their communities, and the wider world. Our location in Otago gives us a distinctive vantage point — grounded in New Zealand's cultural heritage, yet globally connected through our partnerships and alumni network.

Whether you are just beginning your academic journey, advancing your career, or considering how DameCooper can help you achieve your goals, I hope you will find a home here. Our faculty and staff are here to support you every step of the way.

Thank you for considering DameCooper. I look forward to welcoming you to our community.

Professor Brenda Michaels President, DameCooper Institute

Mission & Values

What we stand for.

Our mission is to advance knowledge, empower minds, and serve society through teaching, research, and community engagement.

Academic Excellence

We pursue the highest standards of scholarship, teaching, and research — guided by curiosity, rigour, and a commitment to truth.

Community & Belonging

We foster a welcoming, inclusive environment where students, staff, and visitors feel valued, respected, and supported.

Global Perspective

We prepare graduates for a connected world — through international partnerships, study opportunities, and a globally-minded curriculum.

Innovation

We embrace new ideas, technologies, and ways of thinking — and we encourage our students and staff to do the same.

Sustainability

We are committed to environmental stewardship, to responsible operation of our campus, and to research that addresses real-world challenges.

Student Success

We put students at the centre of everything we do — supporting their academic, personal, and professional growth from enrolment to graduation and beyond.

Our History

Three decades of academic life.

From a small teaching institute in Otago to a globally connected centre of learning — the story of DameCooper is one of steady growth, academic rigour, and community.

1992

Founding

DameCooper Institute is established in Dunedin, Otago, with an initial cohort of students and a small faculty dedicated to teaching excellence.

2001

Academic expansion

New schools of Business and Engineering are established, and the institute begins offering postgraduate qualifications.

2010

International reach

DameCooper welcomes its first substantial cohort of international students and establishes formal partnerships with institutions across Asia, Europe, and the Pacific.

2018

Research growth

Dedicated research centres are established in areas including climate science, health innovation, and digital technologies.

2024

Today

DameCooper Institute continues to grow — with a diverse student body, an expanded campus, and a renewed commitment to sustainable, inclusive, and globally-connected higher education.
