Frequently Asked

Answers to common questions.

If you can't find what you're looking for, our admissions team is always happy to help.

How do I apply to DameCooper Institute?
Applications are submitted online through our admissions portal. You'll need to provide your personal details, academic transcripts, and any programme-specific requirements. Our admissions team can guide you through each step.
What are the entry requirements for programmes?
Entry requirements vary by programme and qualification level. Most undergraduate programmes require a recognised secondary school qualification, while postgraduate programmes require a relevant bachelor's degree. Contact the relevant academic school for specific requirements.
Do you offer scholarships or financial support?
Yes. DameCooper offers a range of scholarships and financial support options for eligible students. Please contact our admissions or student support teams for details on current opportunities and how to apply.
Can I visit the campus before applying?
Absolutely. We encourage prospective students to visit our Otago campus. Guided tours are available Monday to Friday between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM. Please contact us in advance to arrange a suitable time.
Do you support international students?
Yes. Our International Office provides support for international applicants, including guidance on visas, study requirements, and life in New Zealand. Contact international@damecooperinstitute.com for assistance.
