Entry Requirements

What you'll need to apply.

Entry requirements vary by programme and qualification level. Below are the general requirements for each level of study.

Undergraduate

For entry into Bachelor's programmes at DameCooper Institute.

  • Recognised secondary school qualification (NCEA Level 3 or international equivalent)
  • Evidence of English proficiency (IELTS 6.0 or equivalent)
  • Certified copies of academic transcripts
  • Valid passport or identification

Postgraduate

For entry into Master's and Doctoral programmes at DameCooper.

  • Relevant Bachelor's degree from a recognised institution
  • Minimum GPA or equivalent academic standing
  • Evidence of English proficiency (IELTS 6.5 or equivalent)
  • Research proposal (for research-based programmes)

Frequently Asked

Questions about applying?

Here are answers to the questions we're asked most often. Our admissions team is always happy to help with anything else.

How long does the application process take?
Once you submit a complete application, our admissions team typically responds within five working days. Some programmes may require an interview or additional documentation, which can extend the timeline.
Is there an application fee?
Please contact our admissions team for current information on application fees. Fee waivers may be available for certain applicants, including those facing financial hardship.
Can I apply before I've completed my current studies?
Yes. You can apply based on your predicted or interim results. Any offer will be conditional on your final results meeting the programme's entry requirements.
What English language qualifications do you accept?
We accept IELTS, TOEFL, PTE Academic, and several other recognised English proficiency tests. If you have completed previous study in English, this may also be considered. Contact admissions for specific guidance.
How will I know if my application has been received?
You'll receive an automated confirmation email once your application has been submitted successfully. A member of our admissions team will then contact you personally to discuss the next steps.
Are scholarships available?
Yes. DameCooper offers a range of scholarships and financial support options for eligible students. Please contact our admissions or student support teams for details on current opportunities.
